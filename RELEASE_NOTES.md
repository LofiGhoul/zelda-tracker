# Zelda 100% Tracker 1.0.0

The first release of the standalone apps for **Windows** and **Android**. Open your Zelda save file and see exactly how close you are to 100%, with a step-by-step guide for every item and Piece of Heart.

## Downloads

| File | For | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| `zelda-tracker-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | Unzip anywhere and run `zelda_tracker.exe`. The app isn't signed, so SmartScreen may warn you: choose **More info → Run anyway**. |
| `zelda-tracker-android.apk` | Android phones and tablets | Open the file on your device and allow installing apps from that source. |

Linux users can run the GTK desktop app from the source code (see the README).

3DS saves can come from Checkpoint/JKSM or from Azahar/Citra/Lime3DS. The app works out which game a save belongs to by itself.

## Highlights

- **One overall percentage** plus a checklist per category: hearts, items, songs, masks, upgrades, bottles, Gold Skulltulas, Golden Bugs, figurines, rings and more, depending on the game.
- **Step-by-step guides for every check.** Locations and item requirements come from the community randomizers' data, so they match the real game.
- **Heart pieces read straight from the save** in every game where the save records them. That includes all 45 in Twilight Princess and 43 of 44 in The Minish Cap, both checked against real saves.
- **Every save slot gets a tab.** Select all slot files at once (for example `save00.bin`–`save04.bin`).
- **Start page** with your recent saves and a one-tap *Continue*.
- **Live updates on Windows:** the app re-reads your save every few seconds, so you can keep it open next to your emulator.
- **Zelda-styled interface** with a colour scheme for each game.
- **Private:** your saves never leave your device.

## Good to know

- **Which pieces you found isn't always stored.** A Link Between Worlds only stores your heart total, so its heart pieces are a guide list you can tick off yourself; a full 20-heart save shows everything as found. A few pieces in the Oracles, A Link to the Past, Majora's Mask 3D and Ocarina of Time 3D are counted from your hearts and marked *Inferred* (amber check).
- **On Android, open the save again after playing** to see new progress. Automatic updates while you play are Windows-only for now.
- **Not supported yet:** Phantom Hourglass, Spirit Tracks, Tri Force Heroes, the GBA version of A Link to the Past, and the Wii and HD versions of Twilight Princess.
- **Item pictures aren't included.** No game artwork ships with the app.
